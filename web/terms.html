Draft. These terms are a working draft and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer in each launch market before public launch.

1. What WIN ZONE is

WIN ZONE organizes free skill tournaments for 1v1 mobile football. It is not a betting, wagering or gambling service. There is no entry fee, no stake and no subscription.

2. Who can play

You must give accurate details and keep your password private. One person, one account.

3. Prizes

Where a tournament offers a prize, it is described on the tournament page and is provided by the organizer or a sponsor. Prizes may be taxable where you live; that is your responsibility. Prizes are never funded by player entry fees.

4. How results are decided

Results come from the game screens on both players' phones, read by the WIN ZONE app on the device, and decided by our servers. Nobody can enter a result by hand. When the two phones disagree, or a match cannot be verified, an administrator reviews it and their decision is final for that match.

5. Screen capture

To verify a match the app asks for permission to capture your screen while a match is running. A notification stays visible the whole time. The app reads the match score and clock on your phone and sends only that information. No video or screenshot leaves your phone, except evidence you choose to upload for a dispute.

6. Fair play

Do not modify the app, fake match state, harass others, create duplicate accounts, or leave matches to disrupt a bracket. We may disqualify players and close accounts that do.

7. Availability

We aim to keep the service running but do not promise it will always be available. Tournaments can be rescheduled or cancelled by the organizer.

8. Liability
