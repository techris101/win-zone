Privacy

Draft. This policy is a working draft and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer, including against Rwanda's data protection law, before public launch.

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, and your age confirmation.
  • Tournaments: registrations, fixtures and results.
  • Match data: the score, clock and screen state read on your phone during a match, plus timing details needed to verify it.
  • Dispute evidence: only if you choose to upload it. Visible to administrators only.

What we do not collect

No audio, camera, location, contacts or messages. Screen capture is processed on your phone; video frames are not uploaded.

Why we use it

To run tournaments, verify results, prevent cheating and support you. We do not sell personal data. Public pages show your username and results.

How long we keep it

Account and tournament records while your account exists. Detailed match logs are archived or deleted on a schedule. Dispute evidence is deleted after the dispute is resolved plus a short review period.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us through the contact page.

Security

Access is controlled by database rules. Room codes are only visible to the two players in a match. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in readable form.

Service providers

We use hosting and database providers to run the service. A final list and locations will be published before public launch.
