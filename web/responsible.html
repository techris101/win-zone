Responsible play

WIN ZONE is free. There are no entry fees, no stakes and no money prizes. Tournaments are about skill and fun, and they should stay that way.

Keep it healthy

  • Set yourself a time for playing, and stop when it is up.
  • Take breaks between matches. Rest your eyes and hands.
  • Do not let tournaments get in the way of school, work, sleep or family.
  • Losing is part of the game. If it makes you angry or upset, step away for a while.

Tools in your account

Open My matches, then Account and safety:

  • Take a break for 1 day up to 1 year. You leave tournaments that have not started and cannot join new ones until the break ends. A break cannot be shortened, so choose a length you are sure about.
  • Tournaments at the same time: set a limit, for example 1 or 2, so you never join more than you can play.
  • Delete account: removes your login and personal details for good.

Fair play and safety

If an opponent cheats, insults you or does not show up, use Report this player on their page or in the match room. Reports go to the WIN ZONE admins, who can suspend accounts. For a wrong result, open the match room and add screenshots of the result screen.

Need to talk to someone?

If gaming is causing you stress or problems, talk to someone you trust, or a health worker near you. You can also contact WIN ZONE.
