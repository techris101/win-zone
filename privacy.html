Platform Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Data Officer: bigmin00001@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

To provide real-money gaming competitions and comply with age and financial regulations, WIN ZONE collects: username, email address, mobile phone number, date of birth (for 18+ age verification), transaction records, match telemetry, IP addresses, and device identifiers.

2. Payment and Banking Information

All deposits and withdrawals are processed via encrypted connections directly with PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways, mobile money providers, and banks. Sensitive card numbers are never stored directly on WIN ZONE frontend servers.

3. Match Data and Anti-Fraud Telemetry

Match sessions, codes, timestamps, dispute submissions, and result evidence are securely logged to detect cheating, fraudulent claims, or duplicate match requests.

4. User Rights and Data Retention

Users maintain the right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of their personal information, subject to statutory retention obligations required for financial auditing and anti-money laundering regulations.
