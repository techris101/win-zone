1. Strict 18+ Age and Identity Eligibility

WIN ZONE is exclusively available to individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction). Real-money staking and participation require users to undergo identity verification (KYC). Any account discovered to have been registered by an underage person will be immediately suspended and barred from the platform.

2. Nature of the Service: Skill-Based Gaming Competitions

All competitions offered on WIN ZONE are organized peer-to-peer gaming matches where outcomes are determined solely by the skill, dexterity, strategy, and knowledge of the participating competitors. The platform does not host games of chance or casino gambling.

3. Matchmaking and Staking System

Users can select from official predefined stakes of $1, $3, $5, $7, $9, $15, $20, $30, and $50 or propose custom stakes. Selection places the player in an matchmaking queue. The system only confirms a match when another eligible, verified opponent selecting the same stake is available.

4. Platform Commission Structure (10% Rake)

There is no upfront entrance or subscription fee. Both matched players contribute their stake to form the total match prize pool. WIN ZONE applies a standard 10% platform commission to the combined prize pool. The winning competitor is awarded the remaining 90% net payout. For example, two $5.00 stakes yield a $10.00 prize pool, from which WIN ZONE retains $1.00 and credits $9.00 to the verified winner.

5. Escrow and Wallet Protection

Upon match confirmation, the exact stake amount is locked in escrow from each player's wallet balance. Locked funds cannot be used concurrently in other matches or withdrawn until the match outcome is finalized.

6. Dispute Escalation & Fair Play Resolution

In case of score disagreement, disconnections, suspected cheating, or fraudulent submissions, either player may submit an official dispute via the platform's dispute interface within 2 hours of match completion. Platform administrators investigate telemetry, match codes, and screenshot proof to render a binding resolution.

7. Official Inquiries & Support

For regulatory inquiries, dispute escalation, or account assistance, contact the official WIN ZONE administration at bigmin00001@gmail.com.
